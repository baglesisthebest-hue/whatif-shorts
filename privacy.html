Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

WhatIf Shorts is a private tool used only by the owner of the What if? YouTube channel, on their own computer and with their own account.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, it uploads videos to the owner's channel, reads comments on those videos, and posts replies the owner has approved. Comments include the public display name of the person who wrote them.

What it stores

The sign-in token and a copy of recent comments are stored only on the owner's computer. They aren't sent to any other server run by this tool.

What it shares

Data is not sold, or shared with anyone other than the services needed to run the tool. Drafting replies may involve an AI assistant used by the owner, which can see the comment text.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Contact

To ask about this policy or have a comment removed, leave a comment on any What if? video, or use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
